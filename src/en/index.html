PC (mouse)
  • Left-drag the model (outside sphere/gizmo): move the model
  • Left-drag (outside model, sphere and gizmo): rotate camera
  • Right-drag (outside sphere/gizmo): pan camera
  • Hold left button (inside sphere, or on a gizmo ring): rotate bone
  • Hold right button (inside sphere/gizmo): move bone
  • Right-click (inside sphere/gizmo, no movement): switch to parent bone
  • Left-click (yellow center dot): toggle control target
  • Hold left button (on another avatar): select that avatar, then drag to move it
Tablet (touch)
  • One-finger drag (on the model): move the model
  • One-finger drag (outside model, sphere and gizmo): rotate camera
  • Two-finger drag (outside sphere/gizmo): pan camera
  • One-finger hold (inside sphere, or on a gizmo ring): rotate bone
  • Long press (inside sphere/gizmo): switch to parent bone
  • Two-finger drag (inside sphere/gizmo): move bone
  • One-finger tap (yellow center dot): toggle control target
  • Long press (on another avatar): select that avatar, then drag to move it
